## 1. HITL Architectural Imperative

Under statutory disaster response mandates (Disaster Management Act 2005), AI and mathematical optimization systems function strictly as **Decision Support Systems (DSS)**. 

No algorithm possesses the legal authority to order mandatory citizen evacuations, seal emergency shelters, or deploy volunteer personnel. Every solver recommendation must be reviewed, validated, and authorized by a designated Government Incident Commander.

## 2. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) Enforcement

Following the established patterns in [`allocation_service.py`](file:///c:/Users/shubh/Downloads/DIRS/backend/app/ai/optimization/allocation_service.py) and [`routing_service.py`](file:///c:/Users/shubh/Downloads/DIRS/backend/app/ai/optimization/routing_service.py):

```python
def check_government_authorization(user: User) -> None:
    """Enforces strict RBAC: Only GOVT officials can approve or modify plans."""
    if not user or user.role != UserRole.GOVT:
        raise ValueError(
            f"Unauthorized: Role '{getattr(user, 'role', None)}' is not permitted "
            "to approve or modify disaster optimization plans. UserRole.GOVT required."
        )
```

- **`UserRole.CITIZEN`**: View public shelter availability and emergency evacuation guidance. Cannot view unassigned shortfalls or approve plans.
- **`UserRole.VOLUNTEER`**: View self-assigned mission orders and transit routes upon government approval. Cannot modify assignments.
- **`UserRole.NGO`**: Submit available relief supplies and registered volunteer rosters. Cannot approve municipal evacuations.
- **`UserRole.GOVT`**: Sole authorized role permitted to invoke solver runs, approve recommendations, execute manual overrides, or reject proposals.

## 3. Manual Override Protocol & Immutability

When ground reality diverges from modeled conditions (e.g., localized structural hazard, VIP shelter reserve, sudden volunteer unavailability), an authorized official can execute an override.

### 3.1 Override Pre-conditions
1. **Mandatory Operational Justification:** An override request missing `justification_reason` (or containing $< 10$ characters) is rejected with `HTTP 422 Unprocessable Entity`.
2. **Atomic Dual-Table Audit Logging:** Every override transaction atomically inserts:
   - An `OptimizationOverrideLog` record containing:
     - `run_id`: UUID of the parent optimization run.
     - `user_id`: UUID of the authorizing official.
     - `action`: `MODIFY` or `REJECT`.
     - `original_value_json`: Exact snapshot of proposed assignment.
     - `overridden_value_json`: Officer's modified assignments.
     - `justification_reason`: Mandatory justification text.
   - An immutable `AuditLog` entry for cross-system compliance and regulatory inquiry.

## 4. Structured Decision Explainability

Black-box outputs cause decision fatigue and distrust during emergencies. Every recommendation generated by Phase 5F must provide structured evidence fields.
